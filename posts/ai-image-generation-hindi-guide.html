AI Image Generation: Prompt se Poster Tak Complete Guide

Pehli baar AI se image banate waqt mera prompt tha: “ek sundar poster banao business ke liye.” Output aaya — ek chamakta hua abstract shape, jo kisi kaam ka nahi tha. Doosri koshish me maine poora brief likha: product, audience, mood, colours, jagah khaali chhodo, text mat daalo. Output pehli koshish se dus guna behtar tha. Farq model ka nahi tha — farq brief ka tha.

AI image generation aaj itni aam ho gayi hai ki har tool “ek click me poster” ka waada karta hai. Sach yeh hai ki tool image bana deta hai, par kaam ki image banane ke liye aapko wahi basic design soch chahiye jo pehle thi: composition, hierarchy, colour aur message. Is guide me main woh poora raasta dikhaunga — diffusion andar se kaise chalta hai, tool kaise chunein, prompt ke hisse, poster ka step-by-step workflow, text wali dikkat, aur licensing ke woh rules jo log sabse zyada ignore karte hain.

Chhota jawab: AI image tool ek bahut tez junior designer hai jo brief follow karta hai, par context nahi samajhta. Achha output paane ke liye prompt me paanch cheezein do — subject, style, composition, lighting aur output format — aur text/logo AI se banwane ki jagah baad me design tool me daalo. Poster ke liye 3–4 variations banao, ek chuno, aur usme 20% haath se sudhaar karo.

Andar kya hota hai — diffusion ek line me

Zyadatar modern image models diffusion par chalte hain. Training ke waqt model ko lakhon images par “thoda noise jodo, phir noise hatao” sikhaya jata hai. Jab aap prompt likhte hain, model random noise se shuru karke kai step me usse saaf image me badalta hai — har step par woh noise ko thoda kam karta hai aur prompt ke hisaab se detail jodta hai. Isliye pehla step sabse zyada “creative” hota hai, aur aakhri steps detail sudharte hain.

Is mechanics se do practical baatein nikaltain hain. Pehli — seed number. Ek hi prompt aur ek hi seed par lagbhag same image milti hai; seed badalne par bilkul nayi. Jab aapko output pasand aaye, seed save kar lo, taaki chhote badlav ke saath wahi image dobara bana sako. Doosri — guidance scale (kuch tools me “CFG”). Zyada value prompt ko sakhti se follow karti hai par image flat aur over-saturated ho sakti hai; kam value creative par loose ho jati hai. 6–8 ke beech se shuru karo.

Aaj ke models me ek aur cheez jud gayi hai — reference image. Aap ek photo ya moodboard de sakte hain aur model uski style, colour ya composition follow karta hai. Poster ke kaam me yeh sabse bada upgrade hai, kyunki brand ke colours aur layout ko har baar prompt me likhne se behtar hai ek reference dena.

Tool chunna — teen tarah ke options

Har tool best nahi hota; sawaal yeh hai ki aapko kya chahiye. Teen categories hain aur unka faisla aapke kaam se hota hai, brand se nahi.

Chat-based tools (ChatGPT ke andar image generation, Gemini) shuruaat ke liye sabse aasan hain. Aap normal bhasha me baat karte hain, woh prompt sudhaar dete hain, aur iteration conversational hoti hai. Nuksan: exact control kam, aur ek hi prompt par style consistency mushkil.

Design-focused tools (Midjourney, Ideogram, Adobe Firefly) aesthetic aur text rendering me strong hote hain. Poster aur thumbnail ke liye Midjourney ka output aksar sabse “finished” lagta hai. Ideogram text thoda behtar likhta hai, par perfect nahi. Firefly ka fayda yeh hai ki woh commercially safer training data par bana hai — jahan legal team sakht ho wahan yeh matter karta hai.

Local models (Stable Diffusion family, ComfyUI ke saath) poora control dete hain — LoRA, inpainting, custom workflow, aur data kabhi bahar nahi jata. Keemat yeh hai ki setup aur tuning me samay lagta hai, aur achhe results ke liye GPU chahiye. Agar aapko mahine me 200+ images banani hain ya client ka confidential product dikhana hai, local setup ka hisaab ban jata hai.

Mera suggestion: pehle hafte ek chat-based tool par kaam karo, sirf prompt likhna seekho. Jab prompt sense ban jaye, tab apne use-case ke hisaab se design tool ya local setup chuno. Tool badalna aasan hai; brief likhna mushkil — wahi asli hunar hai.

Prompt ke paanch hisse

Ek achha image prompt ek chhota brief hota hai. Use paanch hisson me likho, aur isi kram me — model bhi isi tarah padhta hai. Neeche ek asli poster ka prompt hai jo maine reuse kiya:

Subject: ek chhota skincare serum bottle, kaanch ka, amber colour,
               kapde par rakha hua, subah ki roshni me
      Style: minimal product photography, clean, soft shadows, editorial
      Composition: 4:5 portrait, bottle left third me, right side khaali
                   (text ke liye), top par thoda negative space
      Lighting: soft window light, warm, ek halki si reflection
      Details: water droplets, ek chhota green leaf, muted beige background
      Avoid: text, logo, watermark, extra bottles, hands

      Aspect ratio: 4:5
      Seed: 48213

Dhyan dein ki prompt me “beautiful”, “amazing” jaise shabd nahi hain. Yeh model ko kuch nahi batate. “Soft window light” kuch batata hai. Jitna concrete, utna control. Aur “Avoid” line sabse zaroori hai — model ko batana ki kya nahi chahiye, aksar batane se zyada asar karta hai ki kya chahiye.

Ek cheez jo log miss karte hain: aspect ratio pehle tay karo. Instagram post 4:5, story 9:16, blog hero 16:9, print A4. Agar aap square image banakar baad me crop karoge, to composition barbaad ho jayegi. Prompt me hi ratio likho aur composition usi hisaab se socho.

Poster banane ka workflow — saat step

“Prompt likho, image aayi, daal do” wala tarika amateur output deta hai. Poster ek design hai, aur design me layers hoti hain. Mera workflow yeh hai, aur isme AI sirf ek step par hai.

  1. Brief likho. Ek line me message, audience aur jagah. “Skincare serum ka launch, 25–35 urban women, Instagram post.” Isse aage ka har faisla aasan ho jata hai.
  2. Moodboard banao. 6–8 reference images. Yeh aapke prompt se zyada asar karta hai, kyunki model ko dikhana prompt se saaf hota hai.
  3. 3–4 variations banao. Ek hi prompt par seed badal kar. Pehli image par kabhi mat rukna — pehli image brief ka pehla draft hoti hai.
  4. Ek chuno, phir inpaint karo. Jo hissa galat hai (bottle ka shape, background ka patch) usse mask karke dobara generate karo. Poori image dobara banane se behtar hai.
  5. Design tool me le jao. Canva, Figma ya Photoshop — yahan text, logo, price, CTA daalo. Text AI se nahi.
  6. Hierarchy check karo. 2 second me message samajh aata hai? Mobile par 30% zoom par bhi headline padhne layak hai?
  7. Export karo. Platform ke size me, sRGB me, aur file size platform limit ke andar. Instagram ke liye 1080×1350, quality 85–90.

Step 5 sabse zyada ignore hota hai. Log AI image ko final poster samajh lete hain. Par asli poster me typography, spacing aur brand colours aate hain — aur woh AI image ke andar nahi, uske upar bante hain. AI image aapka background ya hero visual hai, poora poster nahi.

Text aur logo — yahan AI ab bhi kamzor hai

Image models ab letters thode behtar likhte hain, par long text, chhote size ka text aur exact spelling ab bhi risk hai. Hindi/Devanagari to aur bhi. Isliye ek saaf niyam: koi bhi text jo padha jana chahiye, AI se mat banwao. Headline, price, date, address, hashtag — sab design tool me type karo.

Agar image ke andar text chahiye (jaise kisi packet par), to teen cheezein karo: text ko chhota rakho, spelling generate ke baad zoom karke check karo, aur zaroorat par us hisse ko inpaint karo. Model 90% time theek likhta hai, aur 10% time “Serum” ko “Semm” bana deta hai — aur wahi 10% client ki nazar me jayega.

Logo ke saath rule aur sakht hai: AI se logo banao mat, chahe woh kitna hi achha lag raha ho. Logo me exact geometry, ek jaisa scaling aur vector format chahiye hota hai. AI raster image deta hai jise har size par saaf nahi kiya ja sakta, aur usme wahi visual elements aa jate hain jo internet par pehle se hain — trademark ka risk. Logo ke liye designer ya vector tool hi sahi raasta hai.

Legal, license aur disclosure

Yeh hissa boring lagta hai jab tak koi notice na bheje. Teen cheezein pata honi chahiye. Pehli — tool ke terms. Har tool batata hai ki output ka commercial use allowed hai ya nahi, aur free plan par aksar allowed nahi hota. Doosri — training data. Kuch models aise data par train hain jahan copyright ka sawaal khada hota hai; agar aapka client ya legal team risk nahi lena chahta, to Firefly jaisa “commercially safe” claim karne wala tool chuno.

Teesri — disclosure. Kuch platforms aur publications AI-generated image par label maangte hain, aur India me bhi AI content ke labelling par baat aage badh rahi hai. Advertising me yeh zyada matter karta hai: agar image se product ka look badal raha hai (jaise serum ki bottle ka size, colour ya texture), to woh misleading ho sakta hai. Product ki asli photo ko reference ya base rakhna sabse safe tarika hai.

Logos, celebrity chehre, brand ke products aur copyrighted characters — inhe prompt me daalne se bachо. Model “Bollywood actor jaisa” par bhi kaafi nazdeek chehra bana deta hai, aur us image ka commercial use aapki zimmedari hai. Practice simple rakho: apna product, apna mood, apne colours.

Iteration aur consistency — brand ke saath kaam karna

Ek achhi image banana aasan hai. 30 images ka set banana, jisme sab ek jaisa lage, mushkil. Yeh brand ke liye kaam karte waqt sabse bada challenge hai. Iske teen practical tarike hain.

1. Style prompt save karo. Apne brand ka ek “style line” banao jo har prompt me jaye — colours, lighting, camera, mood. Example: “muted beige background, soft window light, editorial product photography, 35mm, shallow depth of field.” Har image me wahi line.

2. Reference image use karo. Ek approved image ko reference ke roop me do. Model uski palette aur lighting pakad leta hai. Yeh style line se zyada consistent hota hai.

3. Seed aur model lock karo. Ek campaign ke liye ek hi model aur ek hi seed family rakho. Model badalne par look badal jata hai, chahe prompt same ho.

Local setup me ek aur option hai — LoRA. 20–30 apni images par chhota training chala kar aap brand ka apna “style model” bana sakte hain. Yeh sabse consistent tarika hai, par isme GPU aur thoda technical samay lagta hai. Mahine me 100+ images banane wale ke liye yeh worth hai.

Mera anubhav

Maine ek local bakery ke liye Diwali ke posters banaye — 8 posters, teen din. Pehle din maine Midjourney me “Diwali sweets, festive, warm” type prompt daale aur jo aaya woh generic stock photo jaisa tha: mithai, diya, rangoli, sab kuch par kahin bhi bakery nahi. Client ne kaha, “yeh toh kisi ki bhi ho sakti hai.” Woh sahi tha.

Doosre din maine tarika badla. Unki asli mithai ki teen photos li, unhe reference ke roop me use kiya, aur prompt me unka brand colour (deep maroon) aur unka actual box daala. Output me wahi mithai dikhne lagi, wahi box, aur background me unki dukaan ki lakdi ki shelf ka hint. Poster ab unka lag raha tha. Teen din me 8 posters ban gaye — par asli kaam AI ne nahi kiya, unki photos ne kiya.

Doosri sikh text ki thi. Pehle poster me maine AI se “Happy Diwali” likhwane ki koshish ki. 4 me se 2 me spelling theek thi, ek me “Diwali” do baar aa gaya, aur ek me font aisa tha jo brand ke saath match nahi karta tha. Uske baad maine rule bana liya: image khaali jagah ke saath generate karo, text Canva me daalo. Poster ka time 40% kam ho gaya, kyunki retry karne ki zaroorat khatam ho gayi.

Nateeja

AI image generation ek brief-following tool hai. Diffusion noise se image banata hai, seed aur guidance usse control karte hain, aur reference image sabse strong control hai. Prompt me subject, style, composition, lighting aur ratio — paanch hisse. Poster ka poora kaam saat step ka hai, jisme AI sirf image deta hai; text, logo aur layout design tool me aate hain. Aur legal side par tool ke terms, training data aur disclosure — teeno check karo.

Shuruaat ke liye ek chhota exercise: apne kamre ki kisi cheez ki photo lo, usse reference bana kar ek poster generate karo jisme right side khaali ho, aur phir Canva me us khaali jagah par headline daalo. Yeh ek exercise aapko AI image aur design ke farq ka woh ehsaas degi jo 50 tutorials nahi de sakte.

Aage padhein: prompt engineering guide — likhne ka structure wahi hai, sirf output text ki jagah image hai; aur shorts workflow — thumbnail aur poster ko video ke saath kaise use karein.

अक्सर पूछे जाने वाले सवाल

AI se banayi image commercial use kar sakte hain?

Tool ke terms par depend karta hai. Paid plans me aksar allowed hota hai, free plans me nahi. Client ke kaam se pehle us tool ke license page par commercial use wali line zaroor padhein.

AI image me text theek kaise likhwayein?

Chhota text rakhein, spelling zoom karke check karein, aur zaroorat par us hisse ko inpaint karein. Behtar tarika: text ke liye khaali jagah generate karein aur design tool me type karein.

Kaun sa tool shuruat ke liye best hai?

Baatcheet wala tool (ChatGPT ya Gemini) sabse aasan hai prompt seekhne ke liye. Aesthetic aur poster ke liye Midjourney, legal-safe ke liye Firefly, poore control ke liye local Stable Diffusion.

Ek jaisi style me 30 images kaise banayein?

Ek style line har prompt me daalein, ek approved image reference ke roop me dein, aur ek hi model aur seed family par rahein. Zyada consistency chahiye to LoRA train karein.

Kya AI se logo banana chahiye?

Nahi. Logo me exact geometry, vector scaling aur trademark safety chahiye hoti hai. AI image ko inspiration ke roop me dekh sakte hain, final logo designer ya vector tool me banwayein.
