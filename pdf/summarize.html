1Upload & set options

The summary will appear here.

2How to use

  1. Choose the PDF you want summarised.
  2. Set how many sentences you want (3–15).
  3. Press Summarise.
  4. Read it on the page, or download it as TXT or PDF.

3Rules & limits

  • The summary is extractive: it picks the most important existing sentences, it does not rewrite them.
  • Nothing is sent to an AI service — the text never leaves your browser.
  • Scanned PDFs have no text to summarise; run OCR PDF first.
  • Short documents (under ~5 sentences) come back almost unchanged.
