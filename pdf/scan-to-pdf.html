2How to use

  1. Tap Take photo on a phone, or pick existing images.
  2. Add as many pages as you need — one photo per page.
  3. Keep “Enhance scan” on for sharper black text.
  4. Press Create scanned PDF.

3Rules & limits

  • Camera capture works on phones and tablets; on a computer use “Choose images”.
  • Enhance mode converts to grayscale with extra contrast — good for text, bad for photos.
  • Each image becomes one A4 page, fitted with a margin.
  • Maximum file size is 50 MB per image.
