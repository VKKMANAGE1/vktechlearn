📄 Word to PDF

Convert a .docx document into a clean, readable PDF.

PowerPoint to PDFExcel to PDFHTML to PDF

1Upload & set options

Only .docx files (Word 2007+).

2How to use

  1. Choose a .docx file (Word 2007 or newer).
  2. Press Convert to PDF.
  3. Paragraphs are laid out on A4 pages automatically.
  4. Download converted.pdf.

3Rules & limits

  • Text and paragraph breaks are kept; images, tables and colours are not.
  • Only .docx is supported — old .doc files must be saved as .docx in Word first.
  • Long paragraphs wrap automatically across pages.
  • Maximum file size is 50 MB.
