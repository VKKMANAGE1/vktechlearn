🔢 Page Numbers

Stamp page numbers on a PDF, with position, start number and format.

WatermarkAdd TextCrop PDF

1Upload & set options

2How to use

  1. Choose the PDF you want numbered.
  2. Pick the position and starting number.
  3. Choose the format and font size.
  4. Press Add page numbers and download.

3Rules & limits

  • Numbers are drawn on top of the page content near the margin.
  • “Page X of Y” counts every page in the file, even if you start at 5.
  • Rotated pages keep their rotation, so numbers may look sideways.
  • Maximum file size is 50 MB.
