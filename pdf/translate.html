🌍 Translate PDF

Translate the text of a PDF into another language and save it as text or PDF.

AI SummarizerPDF to WordOCR PDF

1Upload & set options

The translation will appear here.

2How to use

  1. Choose the PDF you want translated.
  2. Pick the source and target languages.
  3. Press Translate — long files are sent in small chunks.
  4. Read the translation on the page, or download TXT / PDF.

3Rules & limits

  • Text is translated, not layout — the translated PDF is plain text in reading order.
  • The extracted text is sent to the free MyMemory translation API, so avoid confidential documents.
  • The free service allows roughly 5,000 characters per day — split very long PDFs.
  • Scanned PDFs need OCR PDF first.
