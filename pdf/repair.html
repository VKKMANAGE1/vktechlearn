🩹 Repair PDF

Rebuild a damaged PDF: first by fixing its structure, then by rebuilding pages as images.

PDF to PDF/ACompress PDFUnlock PDF

1Upload & set options

2How to use

  1. Choose the damaged PDF.
  2. Press Repair PDF.
  3. The tool first tries a structural fix, then rebuilds pages if needed.
  4. Download repaired.pdf.

3Rules & limits

  • A structural repair keeps text and quality and fixes most index/xref damage.
  • If the structure cannot be read, pages are rebuilt as images and text is lost.
  • Severely truncated files may lose their last pages.
  • Maximum file size is 50 MB.
