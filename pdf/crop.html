✂️ Crop PDF

Trim white margins or wide borders by setting exact crop margins.

Rotate PDFPage NumbersRedact PDF

No file selected yet.

2How to use

  1. Choose the PDF you want to crop.
  2. Enter the margins to remove (in points; 72 pt = 1 inch).
  3. Optionally limit the crop to certain pages.
  4. Press Crop PDF and download.

3Rules & limits

  • Margins are removed from every selected page from the outside inwards.
  • The crop is stored in the PDF's page box — viewers that ignore crop boxes may show the full page.
  • A page cannot be cropped to less than 40 × 40 points.
  • Maximum file size is 50 MB.
