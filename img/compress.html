2How to use

  1. Select one or more images.
  2. Pick a quality level and an optional maximum width.
  3. Press Compress images.
  4. Download the single image, or the ZIP for a batch.

3Rules & limits

  • Output is always JPEG, so transparency is replaced with white.
  • Photos usually shrink 50–80%; screenshots and flat graphics shrink less.
  • Downscaling to 1920 px or less saves the most space for web use.
  • Images are processed on your device, up to 40 MB each.
