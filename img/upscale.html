Enlarge a small image 2×, 3× or 4× with smooth high-quality scaling.

2How to use

  1. Choose the image you want to enlarge.
  2. Pick a scale factor.
  3. Press Upscale image.
  4. Download the larger PNG and compare it at 100%.

3Rules & limits

  • This is high-quality interpolation, not an AI model — it cannot invent missing detail.
  • Scaling happens in small steps, which keeps edges cleaner than a single jump.
  • Output is PNG so no extra JPEG artefacts are added.
  • Very large results are limited by browser memory (about 40 megapixels).
