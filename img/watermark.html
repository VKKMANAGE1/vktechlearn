💧 Watermark IMAGE

Stamp your name, brand or site over a photo with adjustable size, colour and opacity.

Photo editorMeme generatorCompress IMAGE

1Upload & set options

JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 40 MB.
No image selected yet.

2How to use

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Type the watermark text and set size and opacity.
  3. Pick a position, or Tile to repeat it diagonally.
  4. Press Add watermark and download.

3Rules & limits

  • The watermark is burned into the exported image, not stored as a removable layer.
  • Tiled mode repeats a rotated watermark across the whole photo — good for proofs.
  • Keep opacity between 0.2 and 0.5 so the photo stays visible.
  • Maximum input size is 40 MB.
