2How to use

  1. Choose one or more JPG files.
  2. Pick PNG or WebP as the output.
  3. Press Convert.
  4. Download the converted image or the ZIP.

3Rules & limits

  • PNG files are larger than JPGs — the quality stays exactly the same.
  • WebP files are usually 25–35% smaller than JPG at similar quality.
  • Animated GIF creation is not supported here; use an animation editor.
  • Maximum input size is 40 MB per image.
