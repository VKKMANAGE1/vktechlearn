🪄 Remove background

Delete a plain or near-solid background and save the subject as a transparent PNG.

1Upload & set options

JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 40 MB.
Higher removes more shades of the background.
No image selected yet.

2How to use

  1. Choose an image with a plain background (white, grey, a studio backdrop).
  2. Raise tolerance until the background disappears.
  3. Press Remove background.
  4. Download the transparent PNG.

3Rules & limits

  • Works by colour matching from the four corners — perfect for plain backdrops, weak on busy photos.
  • There is no AI cut-out here; hair and soft edges keep some background colour.
  • Output is PNG with real transparency.
  • Images are scaled to at most 1600 px for speed.
