🫥 Blur face

Hide faces, number plates or anything private by blurring or pixelating selected areas.

Crop IMAGEWatermark IMAGEPhoto editor

1Upload & set options

JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 40 MB.
No areas selected yet.

2How to use

  1. Choose a photo.
  2. Drag one or more boxes over the areas to hide.
  3. Choose pixelate or blur, and the strength.
  4. Press Save hidden image.

3Rules & limits

  • There is no automatic face detection — you choose the areas yourself.
  • Pixelate is stronger than blur: it destroys detail instead of softening it.
  • The effect is burned into the exported image.
  • Maximum input size is 40 MB.
