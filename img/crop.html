✂️ Crop IMAGE

Drag a rectangle over the photo to keep just that part, with optional fixed ratios.

Resize IMAGERotate IMAGEBlur face

1Upload & set options

JPG, PNG or WebP, up to 40 MB.
Select a file, then drag on the image.

2How to use

  1. Choose an image.
  2. Drag a rectangle on the preview — the shaded part is removed.
  3. Optionally pick an aspect ratio; drag again to apply it.
  4. Press Crop image and download.

3Rules & limits

  • The crop keeps the original resolution inside the selection, not the preview size.
  • Fixed ratios snap the selection to 1:1, 4:3 or 16:9 while you drag.
  • A selection smaller than 2% of the image is ignored.
  • Maximum input size is 40 MB.
